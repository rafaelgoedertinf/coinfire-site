FAQ
Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You talk to the app or send screenshots/PDFs of your statement. No passwords, no bank login.

What happens to my statement images?

They are sent to the AI, analyzed and deleted. We keep only the result. Your money log lives on your phone.

Is this financial advice?

No. CoinFire is entertainment and financial education. It never recommends investments or loans.

Which languages and countries?

English and Portuguese, any bank and any currency. More languages are coming.
